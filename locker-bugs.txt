Worth explicitly avoiding (found as real
  bugs/anti-patterns in that codebase):
  - checkInPOST()/checkOutPOST() retry with a blocking
    delay(2000) inside a for loop — up to 20+ seconds
    of blocked execution per failed attempt. Directly
    contradicts the "never block loop()" rule already
    in my spec; called it out explicitly as what not to
    do.
  - incrementCheckoutRetryCount() silently discards a
    pending record after 3 failed retries. Fine for a
    kiosk where a human can just try again;
    unacceptable for a door-access event, which is the
    only record someone entered. Confirmed my "retry
    indefinitely, capacity-bounded not attempt-bounded"
    design is the right divergence.
  - _messages.h's getNextPendingMessage() deletes the
    file as soon as it's read into memory, before the
    send is confirmed — a textbook data-loss bug,
    exactly the failure mode this whole feature exists
    to prevent. Added an explicit "never delete before
    ack" rule to the spec, regardless of which storage
    shape gets built.
  - Timestamps/filenames built from millis() rather
    than wall-clock time — resets to 0 on reboot, wraps
    at ~49 days, unsuitable for anything that must
    survive or compare across a reset. Annotated the
    PendingEvent.timestamp field to make clear this is
    deliberately SNTP wall-clock, not millis().
